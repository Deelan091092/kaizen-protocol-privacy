Privacy Policy for Kaizen Protocol

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Kaizen Protocol ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and your choices when you use the Kaizen Protocol mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: information you provide when creating or using an account, such as your email address.
  • App and progress data: information you enter into the App, such as tracker progress, habits, checklists, goals, and other self-improvement activity data.
  • Subscription information: information necessary to provide and manage paid features or subscriptions. Payments are processed by the applicable app store or payment provider; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Technical information: information that may be automatically provided by your device or the services used by the App, such as device type, operating system, app version, diagnostics, crash information, and general usage information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Kaizen Protocol.
  • Save and display your progress and preferences.
  • Provide features associated with your account or subscription.
  • Improve app functionality, reliability, and user experience.
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical problems, fraud, or misuse.
  • Communicate with you when necessary regarding the App or your account.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the App, provide services, process subscriptions, maintain security, or comply with legal requirements.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain your account and records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

6. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact details below. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.
